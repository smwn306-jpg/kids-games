## Remaining known design issue
Reference artwork is still supplied at mixed aspect ratios (for example 360x570 Home and 687x493 Profile). A single image cannot both preserve its aspect ratio and fill every modern phone without either cropping or adding surrounding space. The long-term fix is to create clean 9:16 artwork/background assets and keep names, stars, buttons and other dynamic UI in React Native.
